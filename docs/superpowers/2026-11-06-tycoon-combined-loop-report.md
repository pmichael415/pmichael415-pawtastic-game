# New Daycare (`/daycare-next`) — the first complete combined loop: report

Owner brief of 6 Nov ("Quality target — Last Asylum"). Last Asylum is a quality bar only: nothing of its content,
layout, art or mechanics is copied. Everything here is PAWtastic's own and builds on the daycare that already existed.
Private preview only: no release, no change to the live hub, Stripe, purchases, DNS, app.pawtasticacademy.com or the
other games. Game coins only; core play free; no forced waiting.

**How this was proven.** I (Claude) played a fresh daycare through the real screen in Chromium with iPhone 13
emulation, several times. I looked at every screenshot, wrote down what read well and what didn't, and fixed what play
revealed before playing again. This is **AI play in an emulator**: it is not a person and not a real iPhone, and
game time ran 2× faster than normal between beats. A human playtest and a real iPhone are still pending.

## In short

- **Built:** the combined loop, with five ways to grow, chosen spots, facility stages, personal moments and a living,
  readable daycare.
- **The private preview** has it (version 13). Nothing was released.
- **Playing it changed it.** Eleven problems found by playing were fixed, most importantly "families book what you
  offer". Before that change, a player following the game's own advice stalled for five days.
- **Not yet better than Last Asylum,** and I won't claim it is. The best session ran a daycare to day 6 with 4
  playrooms and a bath, 25 of 30 families delighted and reputation 94. The last session shows a real open problem:
  late-afternoon pickup waves overwhelm a player working alone, families give up, reputation falls and fewer families
  come. That is the first thing to fix next (see "Not done").


## The loop

Care for pups yourself → earn → follow what families ask for → choose how to grow and where → watch the daycare
change → hire to keep up → new pups, new moments → care for them yourself.

## What changed, by the owner's seven priorities

### 1. A living daycare — read it by looking
- **A bubble over each pup.** It says what the pup is doing:
  - ❗ needs you (very unhappy, a scuffle, or its family is upset);
  - ✨ asking for you;
  - 🫧 in the bath;
  - 🎾 being played with;
  - ⏳ waiting;
  - 💤 resting.

  A contented pup shows nothing, so the bubbles that do show mean something. 💤 shows only zoomed in: a row of sleeping
  boarders drowned out the pups that needed you. ❗ and ✨ are drawn larger.
- **Families at the desk.** They wait there at drop-off until check-in, then wave goodbye and walk out. At pickup they
  wait there for their pup. Tapping a family opens their pup.
- **Pups move around.** Idle pups wander their room's free squares (never through furniture). Energetic pups trot more
  often; gentle ones pause longer and lie down.
- **The 2D map** (when 3D isn't available) shows the same bubbles and families.

### 2. Growth you can see
- **Five ways to grow,** in four groups, each a real room or yard built with the ordinary build rules:

  | Group | Option | Opens at |
  |---|---|---|
  | More families | Playroom wing | level 2 |
  | New services | Bath station | level 2 |
  | New services | Boarding wing | level 3 |
  | Comfort | Play yard (play yard, water station and shade tree outside) | level 2 |
  | Your team | Staff room | level 5 |

  Each option shows its cost, what it adds, its staffing, its daily upkeep, what it makes possible and **why you might
  want it**. Buying the same option again improves it instead of duplicating it.
- **Facility stages,** worked out from what's built (never stored, so they can't drift):
  - Starter;
  - Growing (any one way grown);
  - Full-service (daycare, baths and boarding: every PAWtastic service);
  - Campus (all five).

  Reaching a stage gets its own celebration, with what it means and what the next one needs.
- **Families book what you offer** (found by playing, below). A family whose kind of pup has no playroom here doesn't
  drop off: no tap, no charge, no reputation change. They are counted as **asking today**. That count names the
  playroom wing's group ("10 Large & Gentle families asked today — you have no room for them yet"). It also becomes
  the next objective, before hiring.

### 3. Freedom to build
- **You choose where it goes.** Every new room or yard offers up to three spots, each shown as an outline in the 3D
  view (and on the 2D map) before you pay, with where it is in words ("behind the Lobby") and its exact price.
- **Prev and Next** move the camera to each spot.
- **Time waits** while you choose.
- **Build mode** stays for full freedom.
- **Real choices:**
  - more playrooms for the families asking;
  - a bath or boarding wing for income per family;
  - a play yard for happier pups and bigger tips;
  - a staff room for a team that keeps going.

### 4. Hands-on play that stays enjoyable
- **Personal moments.** A settled pup sometimes asks for *you*, with its favourite game: fetch, tug, zoomies, a cuddle
  or a splash.
  - **Answering:** one tap gives hearts, a happier pup, XP, and a +5 tip the family adds at pickup ("Tip — Pepper told
    them about fetch with you").
  - **No pressure:** staff never take moments; at most three are open at once; missing one costs nothing.
- **A chip over the daycare** points at the most urgent thing only you can do. A family kept waiting comes first; a
  moment can wait.
- **Mop floors** is now in a pup's panel when its room is getting dirty, and in a room's panel. Before, playing in 3D
  on a phone, there was no way to mop.

### 5. Dogs with personality
- **The pup's panel** shows its favourite game and its trait ("Loves cuddles 🤗 · Tail-wag tornado").
- **Moments speak in the pup's voice:** "Rocket leans into you for a long, happy cuddle."
- **Movement follows temperament.** The larger relationship system stays for a later milestone.

### 6. A compelling next goal
- **Next** always says what to do now. When nothing is urgent, it names the next step toward the next facility stage,
  with its price and a progress bar ("Next: a bath station — a step to a Full-service daycare").
- **Goal** follows the stages, with the current stage shown ("Growing daycare · stage 2 of 4") and what is still to
  build.
- **Families who asked** are mentioned once per kind of pup a day, with a button to the options.

### 7. Polish
- **Sounds and effects:** a chime and hearts when you answer a moment; a fanfare and confetti for a new stage.
- **A compact receipt.** It shows what the family said, what they paid and the reputation change, with the itemized
  bill one tap away. It waits while a panel or the spot bar is open (it used to cover their buttons).
- **The day report** leads with *running the daycare*. Building is shown as what you invested. The first day used to
  read "Profit −2,289" in red after a good day.
- **Panels and notes:**
  - the pup panel has a Close button;
  - panel close buttons are named "Close panel", because rooms also have a "Close" for business;
  - at most two notes show at once.
- **The tour's new playroom** now joins the building (next to a room, with a door), not out on the lot behind a path.

## The play sessions (AI play: Chromium, iPhone 13 emulation, game time at 2–3×)

`qa/play-session.cjs` plays a fresh save through the real screen like a player:
- it taps pups in the 3D view and uses the chip, panels, team sheet, options card and spot bar;
- it follows the game's own Next objective for the business decisions.

Each run writes screenshots, a video and a log to `qa/out/play-session/`. I reviewed every screenshot.

| Session | Build | What happened |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | before any fixes | 30 of 34 families turned away the first morning. Following the objectives (hire, then a bath) left coins near 300 for days 2–6, never enough for the next room. |
| 2 | + families book what you offer | 4 playrooms by day 3. Running profit +172, +401, +498 on days 4–6. Reputation dipped to 52 on day 3 and recovered to 68–72. |
| 3 | + receipt, report, tour and Next fixes | By day 6: 4 playrooms and a bath station, 25 of 30 families delighted, reputation 94, 957 coins. Running profit +721, −596 (two sign-on fees), +99, +424, +701. |
| 4 | + waiting family first on the chip | Reputation 40. The script missed 60 taps on a pup it couldn't hit, each costing about half a game hour; the script now uses the chip like a player. |
| 5 | final build | No errors. Two playroom wings on day 1 (from "families asked") and a receptionist. **Reputation 51:** 12 families left upset after an hour at pickup, mostly in the 15:00–17:30 wave. Fewer families then came (arrivals follow reputation), so running profit stayed near zero on days 3–5. |

**What this shows:**
- **The loop holds together:** care, the families-asked signal, choosing an option and a spot, watching the daycare
  change, stage celebrations, hiring.
- **Growth is visible:** four playrooms around the lobby by day 3–5.
- **Moments land:** "Rocket leans into you for a long, happy cuddle", with the tip on the bill.
- **The weak point is the end of the day.** A family with a concern (an under-played pup, a dirty room) needs you
  personally; staff never answer complaints, by the audited rules. Several arriving within an hour is more than one
  player can reach in 60 game minutes, which is 30 real seconds at normal speed.
- **Fairness of the test:** the script plays at 2× and misses taps on moving pups, so a person would do better.
  Session 3 shows the same strategy can do well. The problem is still real.

## Independent review

A separate reviewer (Claude Opus) read the whole change and probed it with throwaway tests.

**Fixed, each with a test that failed first:**
1. **The moment tip was paid on visits that charged nothing.** A pup kept safe in the calm room, or a family that
   gave up waiting, still added the 5-coin tip. It now comes only with a charged visit.
2. **A closed playroom (or bath, boarding or staff room) made its families count as "asked".** The card then offered a
   duplicate room. A room you have but closed now counts as yours:
   - families of that kind come and wait;
   - the option says to open it again (free);
   - Next says "Reopen your …".
3. **"Paid -25" on the compact receipt.** It now says "Refunded 25" or "No charge".
4. **The chip stayed live while choosing a spot.** It could open a panel over the spot bar, and if the spot list
   shrank, Build here could build a spot that wasn't outlined. The chip now steps aside, and one spot index drives
   both the outline and the build.
5. **A two-notes-at-once cap I had set applied to every game.** Undone; the other games are off limits.

**Deferred (minor):**
- Affordability is checked against the first spot's price. Prices don't vary by spot today, and the charge guard
  holds.
- Objectives are worked out twice per screen update, and the cache key is broader than needed: 2–4 ms measured, no
  stall.
- Pups walk straight across furniture between free squares, and a busy desk queue can spill past the lobby walls.
- The stage celebration pops right as the tour ends, because the tour's playroom makes the daycare Growing.
- "Close" (a room, for business) and "Close panel" sit side by side in a room's panel.
- Up to three notes can stack over the top of the 3D view in a busy daycare.


## Not done (pending)

- **The evening pickup wave** (session 5): the most important next fix. Options I'd weigh, none applied yet:
  - spread pickup times across the afternoon;
  - a family with a concern stays patient while you are with another family;
  - the attendant hire suggested earlier, so fewer pups end the day under-played.

  Any of these changes audited service rules, so I'd do it as its own small milestone with the balance bots and a
  replay.
- **A human playtest and Phil's iPhone test.** Everything here is AI play in an emulator.
- **Wandering makes pups harder to tap.** The script missed 25–60 taps per session; a person at normal speed will do
  better, but this is worth watching in the iPhone test.
- **Not in this milestone:**
  - the relationship system (kept for later, as asked);
  - events;
  - more expansion types;
  - the yard as a fenced room.
- **No release:** the live hub, Stripe, purchases, DNS, app.pawtasticacademy.com and the other games were not touched.

## Phil's iPhone test (about 15 minutes, private preview)

1. Open the preview and choose **Quick start**; follow the guide. Note whether the two celebrations at the end
   (Level 2, then Growing daycare) feel good or like too much.
2. Watch the first morning:
   - Can you tell what each pup is doing from its bubble?
   - Do families wait at the desk and wave when you check them in?
3. When a ✨ chip appears, tap it and answer.
   - Does the moment feel personal?
   - Did you find the +5 tip on that family's receipt (See the bill)?
4. When Next says "N … families asked today", tap **See options** and choose a **Playroom wing**:
   - Try **Next spot** and watch the outline move.
   - Build at the spot you like.
5. Play to 18:00. Read the day report.
   - Does "Running the daycare" make sense?
6. Keep going a day or two:
   - Hire when Next suggests it.
   - Build a **Bath station** at a spot of your choice.
   - Watch the late-afternoon pickups: is it manageable, or does it pile up (the known problem)?
7. Tell me anything that felt slow, confusing or unfair, and whether the daycare felt alive.


## Decisions made for you

1. **Families book what you offer.**
   - A family whose kind of pup has no playroom doesn't drop off: no charge, no reputation change.
   - They count as asking today, and that drives the Playroom wing and Next.
   - **If wrong:** fewer families visible in the lobby early on; the count is the signal instead.
2. **"Families asked" comes before hiring** in Next: growth before more wages.
   - **If wrong:** the first hire comes later.
3. **The Playroom wing builds a new room** whenever families want one for a kind of pup with no room; otherwise the
   second purchase improves, as audited.
4. **The play yard is outdoor items** (play yard, water station, shade tree), because there is no yard room type.
5. **Unlock levels:** boarding opens at level 3 and the staff room at level 5, matching when boarding beds and staff
   couches unlock.
6. **Stages:**
   - Growing is any one of the five ways;
   - Full-service is a bath plus boarding (daycare, baths and boarding are exactly PAWtastic's services);
   - Campus is all five.
7. **Moment timing** is set by the pup and the day, with no dice, so seeded balance runs are untouched.
   - **If wrong:** a pup's moment comes at a predictable time.
8. **💤 shows only zoomed in,** and ❗ and ✨ are drawn larger.
9. **With a bath room closed,** the card says to reopen it instead of offering a second; an audited test was updated
   to match.
10. **Time waits while you choose a spot,** like level-ups and the day report.
